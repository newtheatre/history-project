Emma studied maths and psychology at Nottingham, performing in New Theatre productions including *Antigone*, *Lysistrata* and *Jerusalem*. After graduating she made her professional stage debut in 2015 as Georgiana Darcy in *Pride and Prejudice* at the Crucible Theatre, Sheffield.

She joined the Watermill Theatre ensemble, appearing in productions and tours of *Romeo and Juliet*, *Twelfth Night* and *The Picture of Dorian Gray* (2017), *A Midsummer Night's Dream* (2019) and *Macbeth* (2020), in which she played Lady Macbeth.

On screen, she took her first television lead as Bella Sway in the AMC+ science fiction series *Moonhaven* (2022), and played Rahima in the second season of Starz's *The Serpent Queen* (2024). Film roles include *Layla* (2024), *7 Keys* (2024) and *Low Rider* (2025).
