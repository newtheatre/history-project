Taken to the Edinburgh Festival Fringe, 5th–21st August 1999 (except the 8th and 15th), at C Too, Venue 4. Presented as "Nottingham University Players". A grand prize draw was run after each performance, tickets £1, with donations welcome.

The company had previewed the show at the New Theatre on 3rd July 1999.
