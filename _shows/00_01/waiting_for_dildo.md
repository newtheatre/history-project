“Welcome to my mind…” A sprawling, rapid-fire comedy of some forty-odd characters
shared between a handful of actors, written and directed by Ben Richards and put
together in a single week.

The programme also credited a cast of “People not acting in this play”: Brian
Blessed, Clive, Geoff the Monkey, Sue Lawley and Carol Smiley.
