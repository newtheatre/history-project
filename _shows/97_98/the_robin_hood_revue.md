Performed at Venue 123 at the Edinburgh Festival Fringe. The cast list is given "in order of merriment". Sketches included "Robert and the Hoods", "Robbie Hood: Space Pilot", "Wanted: Robin Hood Dead or Alive" and "DNA".

The programme carries biographies of the company under the heading "Ye Merrie (Wo)men", noting that Claire McKenzie was an ex-president of the New Theatre, Stephen Norfor was chairman of Act III and lighting manager of the New Theatre, and Dan Burnett was New Theatre sound manager. For most of the cast this was a first trip to Edinburgh.

The back cover plugs the two other New Theatre shows on the Fringe that year: *Hitchcocks Bo\*\*\*cks* at Venue 21, Roman Eagle Lodge, 7–23 August, and *We Should Get Out More* at Venue 101, La Belle Angele, 7–31 August.
