Billed as "a modern love story" and as the first production of the summer term. A New Theatre and Pounds, Dollars & Cents production.

The play runs across five acts, set in mid-October, late November, early December, late January and — for the epilogue — mid-September.

From the author's note in the programme: "It is a concept so entirely new, to fall in love with people you know only as a collection of pixels on a screen… But this, though, *is* 'the new face of love': two people, two screens, and thousands of miles in between them; across a crowded Internet, not a crowded room."

Thanks in the programme go to Stu Scott and Phil Andrews for helping with the set, "Superchef" Mungo Stenning for the pizza, Tom Hayton of *Revolver* magazine, and Adam Guest removal services.
