Billed as "adapted from Middleton & Rowley's great tragedy", under the tagline "Obsession and twisted passion" and the quotation "Blood guiltiness becomes a fouler visage". Tickets £3.00 / £2.50.

The scene is the castle at Alicant, Spain. The play opens with the arrival of Alsemero at the temple in Alicant, where he meets Beatrice and falls in love. Unknown to him, Beatrice is engaged to Alonzo de Piracquo and must shortly be married.

The programme thanks Trevor Pitt and the Nottingham Playhouse "for letting us borrow their guns".
