A one-night-only transfer of the original New Theatre production to the Co-operative Arts Theatre, George Street, Nottingham, at 8.00pm on Tuesday 29th April 1997. Billed on the poster as "an original play by Stuart Scott" and "a story of five students sharing a flat". Tickets £4, concessions £3, with a special offer for group bookings.
