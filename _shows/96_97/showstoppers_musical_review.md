A selection of songs and dances from *Starlight Express*, *Grease*, *Copacabana*, *The Rocky Horror Show*, *Little Shop of Horrors*, *West Side Story*, *Miss Saigon*, *Les Misérables*, *The Blues Brothers* and others. Tickets were £2.00 for members and £2.50 for non-members, with two-for-one on the first night.

**Act 1** — Another Opening (Another Op'nin', Another Show); I Got Rhythm; Next Time You…; One Rock 'n' Roll Too Many; Light at the End of the Tunnel (*Starlight Express*); Fever (*Flashdance…What a Feeling*); Sit Down You're Rocking the Boat (*Guys and Dolls*); Dentist (*Little Shop of Horrors*); America (*West Side Story*); Ain't Misbehavin'; Can't Take That Away From Me (*Shall We Dance*); Copacabana; Potiphar (*Joseph*); Pharaoh's Song (*Joseph*); You and I (*Chess*); Grease Medley; Sweet Home Chicago (*The Blues Brothers*).

**Act 2** — The Heat Is On In Saigon (*Miss Saigon*); I Still Believe (*Miss Saigon*); Little Shop of Horrors; Bring Him Home (*Les Misérables*); Look What Happened To Mabel (*Mack and Mabel*); Herod's Song (*Jesus Christ Superstar*); Man Wanted (*Copacabana*); Could We Start Again Please? (*Jesus Christ Superstar*); Tap Your Troubles Away (*Mack and Mabel*); Anything Goes; Time Warp (*The Rocky Horror Show*); Sweet Transvestite (*The Rocky Horror Show*); Last Night of the World (*Miss Saigon*); Everybody Needs Somebody (*The Blues Brothers*); The Band Play Out (*Five Guys Named Moe*).

The programme thanks Zara Chivers and Dave Bass for the photographs, "Mr. Turtington for the sparkly paint", the Print Shop, Jen Harvie and Dave Halford for use of the Performing Arts Studio, Sherwood Hall for use of their dining room, David Monk for the radio mikes, Andy Muir for his help with the set, Hugh Sweeney for his help with sound, and "Barry Manilow for his inspiration….. Thanks Baz."
