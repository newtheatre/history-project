Billed as a "Galactic Comedy Revue" presented by the New Theatre Headlights Revue, under the tagline "May the Farce be with you!". The programme credits the show as "conceived, performed, and written by" the cast, "and a cast of a thousand and one E. Coli" — the team becoming known as The Headlights Revue Team.

Sponsored, per the programme, "by the Kenco and the Adopt a Kakapo Foundation".
