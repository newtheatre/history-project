A community theatre on George Street, Nottingham, housed in the former George Street Particular Baptist Church building. Now known as the Nottingham Arts Theatre, operated by an educational charity with a very active youth theatre company.

It has two performance spaces: a 274 seat main auditorium and a smaller, newer 50 seat studio theatre.
